About Nusrah

Aid that anchors you to Jannah

Nusrah is a transparency-focused platform that connects donors directly with verified charitable causes.

We don't collect, store, or distribute donations. Instead, we verify campaigns, publish authentic information, and enable donors to contribute directly to the campaign organizer's official account. Our goal is simple: Restore trust in charitable giving through transparency and accountability.

Since our launch, Nusrah has helped connect donors with verified masjid, community, and welfare projects — every rupee tracked from pledge to progress update, with zero platform commission taken from campaign funds.
