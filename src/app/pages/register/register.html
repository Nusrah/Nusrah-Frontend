Nusrah Volunteer Program

Join Our Team & Earn Sadaqah Jariyah

Be the bridge between generous donors and local communities. Help us verify needs, monitor progress, and bring transparency to charitable giving for the sake of Allah.

📋

Roles & Responsibilities

As a Nusrah volunteer, your primary mission is ensuring complete transparency and trust across campaigns.

  • ✓ Verify genuine campaign needs and project details on the ground.
  • ✓ Monitor fund utilization and make sure contributions reach the rightful recipients.
  • ✓ Maintain transparent documentation to build absolute donor confidence.
🕌

Local Masjid Assignment

We believe community work starts locally. Admins will assign each volunteer to their nearest masjid or a masjid of their personal liking and preference.

  • ✓ Coordinate directly with local mosque committees for community welfare.
  • ✓ Seamlessly oversee local community campaigns near your neighborhood.
  • ✓ Foster local unity and charitable engagement close to home.
📊

Ground Work & Digital Updates

You will be provided an exclusive volunteer portal where you can document your field findings and keep donors updated in real-time.

  • ✓ Perform on-site checks on progress, funds, and active requirements.
  • ✓ Use the dedicated volunteer dashboard to upload photos, reports, and progress updates.
  • ✓ Keep donors informed with genuine visual proof and transparent milestones.
✨

Sadaqah Jariyah & Aakhirah Rewards

Doing this good work purely for the sake of Allah SWT brings immense blessings. Every effort you make serves as a continuous charity (*Sadaqah Jariyah*).

"When a person dies, all their deeds come to an end except three: ongoing charity (Sadaqah Jariyah), beneficial knowledge, or a righteous child who prays for them." (Sahih Muslim)
